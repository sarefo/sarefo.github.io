Manual Shift Trainer

A simple model of a manual motorcycle clutch and gearbox. It does not simulate balance or steering.

White needle: engine rpm. Cyan mark: the engine rpm that would match the rear wheel in this gear. Releasing the clutch pulls the needle toward the mark; a big gap gives a lurch.

Where the controls are (typical manual 125)

  • 1Clutch lever (left hand). Pull in to disconnect the engine.
  • 2Front brake lever (right hand). The strongest brake.
  • 3Throttle (right grip). Twist toward you to rev.
  • 4Left switches: turn signal, horn, high/low beam.
  • 5Right switches: starter button, engine kill switch (RUN / OFF).
  • 6Dash: speedometer, green N neutral light, turn indicators.
  • 7Ignition key (position varies by bike).
  • 8Mirrors. Set them before you ride.
  • 1Gear shifter (left foot). Press down for 1st; lift for neutral and the higher gears.
  • 2Rear brake pedal (right foot, far side of the bike, drawn dashed).
  • 3Side stand (left). Fold it up before moving; many bikes cut the engine if it is down in gear.
  • 4Footpeg. Balls of your feet on the pegs.
  • 5Fuel tank. The fuel valve (small dot) is under it on bikes that have one.
  • 6Seat.
  • 7Chain and sprockets (check slack and lubrication).
  • 8Engine and gearbox.

Generic layout. The flyer does not say which Kawasaki 125 the school uses, so check positions on the real bike. Many 125s have 4 gears (1, N, 2, 3, 4); this simulator has 5.
